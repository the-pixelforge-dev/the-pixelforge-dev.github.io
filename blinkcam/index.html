BlinkCam by ThePixelForgeDev

Snap. Share. Gone.

Coming soon to Google Play

BlinkCam is a temporary camera for photos and videos you only need to send once. Take it, share it, and BlinkCam deletes it, so it never clutters your gallery or gets backed up to the cloud.

  • Tap the shutter for a photo, hold it for a video of up to 20 seconds.
  • Share through your phone's share sheet, then the file is deleted automatically.
  • Retake or Delete anytime; nothing is ever saved to your gallery.
  • Selfie camera, flash, timer (3, 5 or 10 s), grid lines, pinch to zoom.
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no internet access.
